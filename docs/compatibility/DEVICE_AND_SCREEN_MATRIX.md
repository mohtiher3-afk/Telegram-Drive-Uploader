Representative classes are used to organize future testing. They are not certified devices.

| Class | Representative characteristics | Install | Startup | Auth | Upload | Background | UI/RTL | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

| Normal phone | Typical phone width and density | NOT TESTED | NOT TESTED | NOT TESTED | NOT TESTED | NOT TESTED | NOT TESTED | Evidence required |
| Large phone | Large width/high resolution | NOT TESTED | NOT TESTED | NOT TESTED | NOT TESTED | NOT TESTED | NOT TESTED | Evidence required |
| Tablet | Wide layout and larger window | NOT TESTED | NOT TESTED | NOT TESTED | NOT TESTED | NOT TESTED | NOT TESTED | Evidence required |
| Foldable | Folded/unfolded configuration changes | NOT TESTED | NOT TESTED | NOT TESTED | NOT TESTED | NOT TESTED | NOT TESTED | Only applicable if hardware is available |
| Low-memory device | Constrained memory under queue/background pressure | NOT TESTED | NOT TESTED | NOT TESTED | NOT TESTED | NOT TESTED | NOT TESTED | Evidence required |

## Screen Checks

Future evidence must cover small, normal, large, and tablet widths and verify no clipping or overlap in onboarding, upload cards, search/destination results, settings, dialogs, long filenames, long chat names, progress states, and navigation. Arabic and English, light and dark themes, and increased font scale must be included.

No screen-size claim is made from source inspection alone.
