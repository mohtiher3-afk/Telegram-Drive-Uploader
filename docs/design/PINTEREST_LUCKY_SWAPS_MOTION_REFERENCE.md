# Pinterest Reference — Lucky Swaps Dashboard Motion Language

**Reference:** https://www.pinterest.com/pin/606086062398747903/

## Observed visual evidence

The reference presents a high-contrast dark dashboard with a wide left rail, a bright product wordmark, a compact metric row, a vertical progress/level panel, a central scoreboard-like activity list, a right-side stream of status updates, and a single luminous blue-violet organic accent sweeping through the layout. A lower callout card concentrates the strongest illumination; smaller surfaces remain dark and quiet.

## Full-theme motion direction

Adopt a three-speed vocabulary: **micro** (selection/press, 120–160 ms), **state** (connection/status substitution, 180–240 ms), and **spatial** (page/content entrance, 260–360 ms). Motion will be interruptible, use existing `AppMotion` tokens, and snap when Android motion is disabled. It will never invent upload progress, success, errors, notifications, or activity.
